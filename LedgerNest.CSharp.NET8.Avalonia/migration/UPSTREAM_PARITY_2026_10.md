### Invoice void and payment-output batch

- Central `InvoiceStatusRules` handles Voided/Declined aliases, financial query eligibility, outstanding balance and payment QR eligibility. Financial reports and previous customer balances exclude voided invoices, while lists and CSV retain their status.
- `InvoiceLifecycleService` repeats View/Update authorization and payment-history checks at the service boundary. Voiding and stock reversal commit in one SQLite transaction. Existing stock movements are retained; compensating movements restore exact decimal quantities once. Documents predating the stock ledger do not gain inferred stock. No schema change.
- Payment entry checks Update permission and rejects voided records against the database, including callers holding stale UI records. Payment writes run transactionally. The existing AXAML payment-history dialog has a read-only voided state and does not label voided invoices as paid.
- UPI amounts use outstanding balance. PDF payment QR is suppressed for settled, voided, trashed and non-invoice documents; invoice-number QR remains independent. Common PDF/thermal output retains status; HTML balances follow the same policy.
- Focused tests cover stock reversal, duplicate void, payment aggregate/history restrictions, denied direct service calls, injected database failure rollback, stale payment requests, report/CSV behavior, six PDF templates and actual AXAML payment-history visibility. Final batch validation: 29 passed, zero failed/skipped; TRX in `artifacts/upstream-tests/void-final`. Build of the Desktop test project and dependencies passed.
- Still pending for full upstream decline parity: quotation conversion/reversion links and the Hide declined management filter. This batch does not claim the entire audit is complete.
